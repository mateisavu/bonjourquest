Controls & guidance

Keyboard

  • Focus: focus the game canvas to use movement keys. Tab moves to page controls.
  • Move: ← / → or A / D.
  • Jump: Space. Release early for a shorter jump.
  • Climb: hold ↑ / W or ↓ / S beside a ladder.
  • Gate: press E near a glowing gate.
  • Pause / resume: Escape or the Pause / Resume button.

Touch

  • Move: hold the left or right button below.
  • Jump: use Jump; release early for a shorter jump. Hold movement and Jump together to cross gaps.
  • Climb: hold Climb or Down beside a ladder.
  • Gate: use the Gate button near a glowing gate.
  • Pause / resume: use the Pause / Resume button above the scene.

Gates & progress

  • Reach the glowing gates to test your French. Gates also open the quiz automatically when reached.
  • Listen if you like, then answer correctly to unlock the next section. Quizzes have no timer or heart penalty.
  • Checkpoint gates save automatically. Gentle mode prevents barrel damage.
